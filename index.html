FAQ

Is my CV uploaded anywhere?

No server, no account, no analytics. Parsing, tailoring, and the tracker all run in your browser and save only to this device's local storage.

Will it write lies into my CV?

No. It reorders and re-emphasizes what's already in your CV, weaves in the post's keywords, and marks anything you should learn as [bracketed placeholders] for you to fill honestly.

Does the URL fetch work on every job board?

It tries a public reader proxy; JobStreet/LinkedIn often block bots, so pasting the post text is the reliable path (it's 10 seconds). The button never blocks you — paste always works.
